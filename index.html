Note 01 — Current build

We're building an electric hydroplane for the American Society of Naval Engineers' Promoting Electric Propulsion competition — a race built to accelerate electric propulsion research in an industry that still runs almost entirely on gas.

The bigger project is the club itself: making it a place where students can come together and build their careers without paying for extra engineering class or shop time.

Electric Boat Racing Club

President

Elected after working up through the design team and a term as secretary — improving the boat's aerodynamics, leading hull design on "Big Dawg I," and designing, prepping, and painting "Seadog II," which took 1st place at UConn's ECE DEMO Day in 2025 alongside senior engineers. Now running the club and its ASNE electric hydroplane program.

General Dynamics Electric Boat

Engineering Intern — Quonset Point, RI

Managing delivery and preventative maintenance scheduling for the industrial gas systems critical to continuous Class 1 submarine production. Working with senior engineers to solve the system roadblocks that halt production, and building the knowledge it takes to scale industrial defense manufacturing into more buildings.
