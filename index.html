Note 01 / Current build

We're building an electric hydroplane for the American Society of Naval Engineers' Promoting Electric Propulsion competition. The race exists to speed up research into electric propulsion in an industry that still runs on gas.

As president, my plan for this school year is to put members on real engineering work while they're still in school. Hulls, powertrains, and the failures in between are the parts of the job you don't get from a syllabus.
