How it works

One person from the first sketch to the last stone.

  1. 01

    Free consultation and sketch

    Jim walks the property with you, listens to how you want to use the space, and sketches it on site. You get an estimate of labor and materials and samples to look at in your own light.

  2. 02

    Digital design

    For projects that benefit from it, we build a 3D rendering so you can see the layout, materials, and grades before anything gets dug up. Changes are easy at this stage.

  3. 03

    Build, with Jim on site

    Our own crew does the work. No subcontractors. Jim is on the job from excavation through cleanup, so the person who designed it is the person checking every course.

    Warranty
