About

Our story

A small company on purpose.

Natural Choice Landscaping is a hardscape and landscape design-build company with locations in Tolland and Manchester, Connecticut. Jim MacGillvary founded it in 1997.

[COMPANY HISTORY: confirm with owner. How Jim got started, the first jobs, how the company grew, what a typical season looks like.]

We have stayed the size we are so the owner can be on every job. Our own crew does the work. We do not subcontract it out, and we do not hand your project to a foreman you have never met.

Most of our business comes from referrals: past clients who send their neighbors, friends, and family. That is the only kind of growth we want, because it only happens when the work holds up.

[PHOTO: portrait of Jim MacGillvary]

Jim MacGillvary, owner

On site from design through completion.

Jim does the consultation himself. He walks the property, asks how you want to use the space, and sketches the plan with you. When the crew shows up, he is there too, and he stays until the job is finished.

That means one person is responsible for your project from start to finish, and you always know who to call.
