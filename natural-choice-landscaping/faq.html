Before you hire anyone

What to ask any contractor.

You are probably getting more than one estimate. Good. Ask every contractor these questions, including us, and compare the answers.

  1. Are you licensed in Connecticut?Get the HIC number and look it up with the CT Department of Consumer Protection.
  2. Can I see a certificate of insurance?Liability and workers' compensation. Ask for the certificate, not just a yes.
  3. Who will be on my job every day?Will the person who designed it be there, or will a crew you have never met show up?
  4. Do you use subcontractors?If so, for which parts of the job, and who is responsible for their work?
  5. What goes under the patio or wall?Ask what base material is used, how deep it goes, and how it is compacted. This is where most failures start.
  6. How will you handle drainage?Where will the water go, and will it end up against the house or in the neighbor's yard?
  7. Exactly what materials are in the estimate?Product, color, and thickness, in writing, so you can compare estimates fairly.
  8. What is the warranty, and is it in writing?What is covered, for how long, and what is not.
  9. Can I see finished work or talk to past clients?Recent projects near you are the best proof.
  10. What is the payment schedule?Know what is due before the job starts, during, and at completion.
  11. Who calls before you dig, and who handles any permits?Utility mark-outs are required before excavation. Permits may be needed for some walls, kitchens, and pool areas.
