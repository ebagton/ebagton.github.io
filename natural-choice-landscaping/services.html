Patios

Paver, bluestone, and natural stone patios sized to how you actually use the yard. Jim lays it out with you on site, so the table fits, the grill has room, and the steps land where you walk. Most patio problems start below the surface, so we take the base seriously.

See patios photos

[PHOTO: curving front walkway to entry]

Walkways

Front walks, side paths, and stepping stones that tie the house, driveway, and yard together. We match the material to the house and the patio so it looks like it was always there.

See walkways photos

[PHOTO: paver driveway, front of house]

Driveways

Paver driveways and driveway aprons built to carry vehicles through Connecticut winters. A driveway is the first thing people see, and a paver drive can be repaired a section at a time instead of repaved.

See driveways photos

[PHOTO: block retaining wall on a sloped yard]

Retaining Walls

Retaining walls that turn a slope into usable yard. Block, natural stone, or boulder, built with the drainage and footing the wall needs to stay straight. Tall walls or walls near structures may need engineering, and we will tell you up front if yours does.

See retaining walls photos

[PHOTO: sitting wall with capstone and columns around patio]

Sitting Walls & Columns

Sitting walls give a patio an edge and extra seating without more furniture. Columns mark an entry or frame a driveway, and both can be wired for lights.

See sitting walls and columns photos

[PHOTO: stone steps up a grade]

Stairs

Steps up a grade, front stoops, and stairs off the deck down to the patio. We set them with even risers so they are comfortable to walk and safe in the dark.

See stairs photos

[PHOTO: paver pool deck with sitting wall]

Poolscapes

Pool decks, coping, and the patios, walls, and plantings around them. We plan the whole area together so the pool, the lounge space, and the walkways back to the house work as one.

See poolscapes photos

[PHOTO: outdoor kitchen with grill and stone counter]

Outdoor Kitchens

Built-in grills, counters, and bar seating finished in stone to match your patio. We plan the layout around how you cook, where people gather, and where gas and electric need to run.

See outdoor kitchens photos

[PHOTO: stone fire pit at dusk]

Fire Features

Fire pits and outdoor fireplaces, wood or gas, built to be the spot everyone ends up. We size and place them for seating, wind, and distance from the house.

See fire features photos

[PHOTO: stone waterfall or bubbling fountain]

Water Features

Waterfalls, pondless streams, and bubbling stones. The sound of moving water covers road noise and makes a patio feel more private.

See water features photos

[PHOTO: bluestone steps and stone veneer on foundation]

Veneer & Bluestone

Natural stone veneer for foundations, columns, and kitchen bases, and bluestone for patios, treads, and caps. Bluestone is a New England standard for a reason: it looks right on old houses and new ones.

See veneer and bluestone photos

[PHOTO: boulder outcrop and boulder steps]

Boulders

Boulder walls, boulder steps, and single stones set as accents. Placed well, a boulder looks like it came out of the ground where it sits.

See boulders photos

[PHOTO: backyard patio and walls lit at night]

Landscape Lighting

Path lights, step lights, under-cap wall lights, and uplighting on trees and the house. Lighting lets you use the patio after dark and makes steps and edges safe to walk.

See landscape lighting photos

[PHOTO: planting beds along new patio]

Plantings

Trees, shrubs, and perennials that soften the stonework and fit the site's sun and soil. We plant as part of the project, so the beds are planned with the hardscape, not added at the end.

See plantings photos

[RENDERING: 3D design of patio, walls, and kitchen]

Digital Designs

3D renderings that show your project before we dig. You can see the layout, materials, and grades from different angles and make changes on screen, where they cost nothing.

See digital designs photos

[PHOTO: granite posts at driveway entrance]

Wood Products & Granite Posts

Granite posts for entries, mailboxes, and post and rail fencing, plus wood products that pair with the stonework. Granite posts are a classic Connecticut detail and they last.

See wood products and granite posts photos
